***FEATURES***

original_area: no of pixels in the cell mask 
aspect_ratio: The original bb is (min_y, min_x, max_y, max_x): (max_x - min_x) / (max_y - min_y)

circularity: This measures how close to a perfect circle the mask. (4 * pi * area) / (perimeter^2).
solidity: Solidity is the ratio of the mask's actual area to the area of this convex hull.
mean_intensity: uses the cell mask to select only the pixels from the intensity_image that belong to that cell
std_intensity: After selecting all the pixels within the cell mask, we calculate their standard deviation. Checks uniformity of texture
